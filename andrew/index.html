01 · After the summary

A second e-mail, from you: "Andrew will read you your plan."

A few minutes after your client gets their written summary, a second e-mail arrives from support@, signed by you, with replies coming to you. One button: Listen to your plan.

  • Nothing for you to do: it goes by itself
  • Your draft e-mail, your fact find and the Guardian form are untouched

02 · Private, and made for the car

Their date of birth opens it. Then they just listen.

The link is private and expires. If their date of birth is on the fact find, they enter it before anything plays. Then one tap, and Andrew reads the plan in about three minutes, with the cards lighting up as he speaks.

It is one recording, so it keeps playing with the phone locked, on the drive home.

03 · Your advice

Your plans, your amounts, your reasons. In plain words.

Andrew never writes a recommendation. He reads yours, exactly as you wrote it on the fact find, and adds only facts from it: what the family spends, cover that is only through work, a spouse with no cover of their own, what university will cost by then.

Sample
04 · The moment they finish

They heard it all. You get: "Call now."

When your client reaches the end, you get an e-mail straight away: "Jordan Mitchell just listened to the whole plan. Call now." It is fresh in their mind. Ask what they thought, answer any question, and ask them to confirm.
