FY27 · 1 October 2026 – 30 September 2027

Your year needs a flight plan.

Seven stages, about fifteen minutes. Start with what your life costs, work back to the API that pays for it, then decide how you’ll work. File it, and every time you sign in you’ll see exactly where you stand.

Your API goal and your progress against it go on the branch wall, in the race to finish first. Everything else in your plan — your budget, your income, your answers — is seen only by you, your unit manager and the branch manager. At the blastoff the big screen only shows that you’re GO.
