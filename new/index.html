10 · Before 31 December

The year-end annuity working.

Up to TT$60,000 of approved pension, approved deferred annuity and NIS contributions comes off taxable income each year, and unused room does not carry forward. From 2026, income from an approved deferred annuity maturing between 50 and 70 is tax-free.

Open it with the client: their income, NIS and pension in, and it shows the room still unused, the tax it saves and the working line by line.
