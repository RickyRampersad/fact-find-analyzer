Your retirement deduction resets on 1 January.

Each year, up to TT$60,000 of what you put into an approved pension, an approved deferred annuity and National Insurance comes off your taxable income. Whatever you don't use this year, you lose. And from 2026, the income an approved deferred annuity pays you in retirement is tax-free.

Why an approved deferred annuity, this year

Tax relief going inPremiums to an approved deferred annuity count toward the TT$60,000 yearly deduction, together with your pension contributions and NIS.
Tax-free coming outFrom 1 January 2026, income from an approved deferred annuity bought by a resident and maturing between 50 and 70 is exempt from income tax.
Use it or lose itThe cap is yearly. Room you leave unused on 31 December does not carry forward.
Know the conditionsCorporate annuities fall outside the new exemption, and surrendering early stays taxable. Your advisor will walk you through the plan's own terms.
